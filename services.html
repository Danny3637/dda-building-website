How we work

Five stages, from first call to final snag list

  1. Enquiry and site survey

    We walk the space, check the drawings against what's actually there, and confirm the specification is buildable before we price it. No charge, no obligation.

  2. Specification and programme

    We confirm partition types, board build-ups, fire ratings and acoustic requirements against the architect's specification, and agree a programme that sits alongside the other trades on site.

  3. Materials and mobilisation

    Materials are ordered against the confirmed specification — not a generic estimate — and delivered to a sequence that matches the installation programme, not the loading bay's convenience.

  4. Installation

    Setting-out, framing, boarding, insulation and finishing run as a coordinated sequence of subcontractor gangs, with a dedicated DDA supervisor on site throughout. Weekly progress photos are shared through our project portal.

  5. Snagging and handover

    Raking-light inspection, a joint walk-round with the client or main contractor, a closed-out snag list, and fire-stopping documentation handed over for the building control file.
